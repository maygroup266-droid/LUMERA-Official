THE ART OF FRAGRANCE

Make Every Scent Last

A fragrance deserves the right application and care. Follow these professional tips to help maintain its performance and character.

01 / PREPARATION

Start with Clean Skin

Apply fragrance to clean, dry skin. This provides a fresh base for the fragrance to develop.

02 / MOISTURISATION

Moisturise Before Application

Apply an unscented moisturiser before your fragrance. Hydrated skin can help the scent remain noticeable for longer.

03 / APPLICATION

Focus on Pulse Points

Apply to suitable pulse points, such as the wrists and sides of the neck, where warmth can help diffuse the fragrance.

04 / TECHNIQUE

Avoid Rubbing

Allow the fragrance to settle and dry naturally. Rubbing the application area is unnecessary.

05 / APPLICATION

Apply Evenly

Follow the product's application guidance and use an appropriate amount for the setting and occasion.

06 / STORAGE

Store Your Fragrance Properly

Keep your perfume in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, excessive heat and temperature fluctuations.

Fragrance longevity may vary depending on formulation, skin chemistry, climate, application method and environmental conditions.
